# Content Archetypes

Six content archetypes for the Instagram Carousel Skill. The archetype is what the post IS (its job, its structure, the outcome it wins). The style pack (`style-packs.md`) is what it LOOKS like. Archetype is picked first, at Step 0.25, outcome-first.

Adapted from @growithalex's six carousel archetypes (IG post DbJVVQ8k68m, July 2026), mapped to Gridiron Warrior. Pack names below follow `style-packs.md`; if the two files ever diverge on a pack name, `style-packs.md` wins.

---

## The selection rule: outcome first

Decide what the post should WIN, then pick the archetype that wins it. Posting a Confession and wondering why nobody saved it is not a quality problem, it is a format problem.

| You want... | Archetype |
|---|---|
| Saves (reference material) | The System or The Formula |
| Highest save rate | The Vault |
| Shares (sent to a teammate) | The Teardown |
| Comments + DMs (pipeline warm signals) | The Template |
| Follows (relationship) | The Confession |

For the depth strategy, comments and DMs outrank saves: they start the conversations the Daily Pipeline answers with the Golden Question. When two archetypes both fit, take the one that produces a conversation.

## The six archetypes

### 1. The System (the Playbook)

Hand over a whole process, numbered, with a rule you don't break. Wins saves, because nobody can hold six rules in their head.

- **Structure:** cover claim, thesis, one rule per slide, the trap (what breaks the system), CTA.
- **GW angles:** "6 rules for inseason lifting. No exceptions." "The 5-step gameday warmup."
- **Pack fit:** Mono Series (numbered rhythm), The Case (argument-led), Newsprint Bauhaus.

### 2. The Formula (the Blueprint)

Break one skill into named parts. Naming the parts is what makes it yours instead of advice they've heard before. Wins saves and authority.

- **Structure:** the skill, then each named part gets a slide, then how the parts chain, CTA.
- **GW angles:** name the phases of contact prep, the parts of a speed session, the anatomy of a summer week. "High Expectations + High Support" is already a Formula.
- **Pack fit:** Editorial Long-Form, The Case.

### 3. The Teardown (the Autopsy)

Show the before and the after and change ONE thing. Wins shares, because people send it to someone who needs it.

- **Structure:** the "before" (real or composite, never a named coach without consent), what's wrong (one thing only), the fix, the after, CTA.
- **GW angles:** Film Study as a carousel. Tear down a random YouTube program, a bad warmup, a template program vs a built program.
- **Pack fit:** The Case, Newsprint Bauhaus (myth-vs-fact energy).

### 4. The Vault (the Collection)

A collection too big to remember: 20 finishers, 15 KPIs, 12 drills. Highest save rate of any archetype, because it's impossible to memorize.

- **Structure:** cover promises the count, items grouped 3 to 5 per slide, one slide on how to actually use them, CTA.
- **GW angles:** mine the archive. 900+ letters, transcripts, drill libraries. "20 inseason finishers." "15 numbers I track for every athlete."
- **Pack fit:** Mono Series, Editorial Long-Form.

### 5. The Template (the Fill-In)

Not a post they read, a thing they USE. Wins comments and DMs, because using it means asking you for it. The single best archetype for the pipeline.

- **Structure:** the problem, the blank template shown on-slide, one worked example, comment-trigger CTA.
- **GW angles:** "Fill in your inseason week, 7 slots." "Your weight room depth chart." Pair with a freebie (gw-freebie-forge) as the fulfillment asset.
- **Pack fit:** Editorial Long-Form, Paper Minimal.

### 6. The Confession (the Scar)

The honest admission with the lesson attached. Wins follows and comments, not saves. Share from a scar, healed, with the takeaway. Never a wound that's still bleeding.

- **Structure:** the admission, what it cost, what changed, the lesson, soft CTA (follow, not sell).
- **GW angles:** 2019 at Merrimack, "my programming was missing a gear." The notebook years. Scott's voice already goes vulnerable and philosophical; this archetype is that, structured.
- **Pack fit:** Asphalt Editorial, Paper Minimal.

## Comment-trigger CTA

Available to every archetype, default for The Template.

Pattern: "Comment WORD and I'll send you X." X is a real asset: a freebie PDF, the template, the full list.

- **Fulfillment is manual.** Scott replies to each comment by DM himself. No comment automation is wired; do not promise instant delivery. Manual is the point: every comment is a warm signal, and an open DM is where the Golden Question lives.
- **The asset must exist** before the carousel ships, or get built alongside it (gw-freebie-forge). Never tease an asset that doesn't exist.
- **One word, uppercase, easy to type:** "PLAYBOOK", "TEMPLATE", "VAULT".
