# Teaching Readiness

Feeder F4, 2026-09-10. Before the writer commits to a teaching form, it checks that the ingredients for that form exist in permitted sources. A comparison needs two real alternatives. A decision guide needs actual criteria. A worked example needs the steps and an honest result. A checklist needs checks, not slogans. When the ingredients support a simpler lesson, the simpler lesson wins. When an essential piece is missing, the check names it instead of inventing it.

This page adds the early check and its receipt. It does not replace the drafting rules in [rhetorical-approaches.md](rhetorical-approaches.md), [visual-teaching.md](visual-teaching.md), [save-reference-slides.md](save-reference-slides.md), [share-led-decks.md](share-led-decks.md), or the action policy in [copy-record.md](copy-record.md). Whether an ingredient is trustworthy is [claim-boundaries.md](claim-boundaries.md)'s job.

## Ingredient checklist

Minimum material per teaching option. Nothing here says a post must include all of them.

| Teaching option | Required material | Never substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Observation | A supported situation or pattern, the detail that matters, a justified response | An invented witnessed scene, or "every coach does this" |
| Demonstration / worked example | Defined input, the steps, a supported or visibly illustrative output, why the steps matter | Made-up results or doses presented as observed |
| Decision guide | A real choice, the distinguishing conditions or questions, a next action per shown branch | Arbitrary thresholds, or a universal rule from one case |
| Correction | A documented misconception, attributed position, or explicitly posed assumption; its limit; a supported alternative | An imaginary incompetent opponent, or "everyone is wrong" |
| Comparison | Two genuine alternatives, a shared context, a declared dimension, real trade-offs | Different populations or units dressed as a fair before/after |
| Personal lesson | Scott's original attributable experience and reflection, meaning preserved | A summary turned into first-person dialogue or emotion |
| Save checklist / reference card | A named future task, concrete checks or fields, enough context to work without the caption | Inspirational bullets, empty unlabeled blanks, a DM-gated answer |
| Share-led explanation | Plausible sender and recipient, a shared situation, understandable perspectives, a joint next move | Tag bait, humiliation, a conflict invented to justify sharing |
| Conversation-led | A specific answerable question grounded in the lesson, and a real response path where one is promised | Fake demand, an empty engagement question, an unstaffed resource promise |
| Conversion-led | A verified current offer that fits the reader's problem, an honest next step, a working destination | A stale offer, a fabricated result, a sale forced onto an unrelated lesson |
| Visual sequence | A supported order; sourced durations only when showing elapsed time | Spacing or bar lengths that imply invented timing or causality |
| Annotated artifact | A permitted real artifact or a clearly labeled illustration, readable elements, useful callouts | Private athlete data, a fake screenshot, an invented outcome shown as real |

An illustrative example can teach mechanics without pretending to report a case. Label it wherever a reader could mistake it for observed data. A hypothetical label never licenses unsupported safety advice or an invented physiological threshold.

## Three outcomes

Editorial outcomes, not queue statuses:

- **Ready.** The available ingredients support the promised payoff in the proposed action and approach.
- **Ready with a supported alternative.** Another allowed form teaches the same useful lesson without inventing material or overriding an explicit instruction. Record the rejected option and the missing ingredient.
- **Needs material.** The missing ingredient is essential. Name it exactly and stop that draft through the repair path below.

Missing never becomes "low confidence, continue as if the example exists." The absence of a diagram or photo is not evidence that a useful prose lesson is unready.

Automatic choices may move to the next feasible action under the brief policy in copy-record.md. When Scott explicitly requested save, share, or a form, try a supported repair inside that request first; if it stays unsupported, keep the saved master and polish note untouched and report the missing material. Never silently substitute a different intent. A promise can narrow when the system chose it, but the topic cannot become a different lesson under the same identity.

## Where the check runs

**Seed.** After the claim boundary and before scoring, the body sketch says which ingredients exist and which do not. A bullet starting `Needs:` names a required ingredient the sources lack. The seed distinguishes "we have the decision criteria" from "someone must supply the criteria." No new label, no new schema key.

**Forge, before slides.** Recheck the current inputs; the seed is a proposal. Follow the installed order: feasible action, supported rhetorical approach, optional visual. For each variant, list the ingredients the chosen form needs, mark each as present (with its source locator or F2 claim id) or missing, and pick the outcome. Two variants need not differ when differing would require invented material. Readiness informs the F3 source-support and payoff reasons; it adds no score.

**Final draft and rewrites.** Verify the promised ingredients actually appear in the draft. A checklist available in the source but absent from the post fails. A save card must work when screenshotted, a share post must help the recipient, a diagram must teach its declared relationship. Action or copy rewrites recheck the changed teaching. Style-only restyles preserve text and meaning. A saved manual edit makes the receipt stale; it never authorizes an automated rewrite.

## Bounded missing-material search

Use the sources already loaded. If one specific required ingredient is missing, name it first, then permit one targeted lookup in authorized GW material of at most two additional documents. Reuse documents F2 already opened; budgets do not stack. No new research pass, no account, no question to Scott overnight. When the ingredient stays missing, choose a supported alternative where the brief policy allows, else record needs material.

## Receipt

Record in the pack-only THE MESSAGE block, per carousel, after the CTA line:

```
- Teaching readiness: ready | ready with a supported alternative | needs material
- Promised payoff: [the decision, tool, or lesson the reader receives]
- Ingredients used: [short description with source locator or claim id; one per ingredient]
- Missing/rejected option: [only when an option was rejected: the option and the exact missing ingredient]
- Selected approach/action: [the brief's values, not a second choice]
```

THE MESSAGE stays immediately before PULLED FROM THE BRAIN, which stays last. These are production notes. Illustrative labels that protect readers still appear on the slide under existing rules. No new JSON master, no database, no format catalog. Legacy packs have no receipt and get none.

## Repair path

Same shape as the F2 blocked-topic procedure: no `_inbox/<slug>/` folder (a created folder retires the idea as forged), the draft plus `NEEDS-MATERIAL.md` under `Deliverables/_pending-drafts/needs-material/<slug>/`, the backlog row `skipped` with `skip_reason: "needs-material (F4): <ingredient>; draft at ..."`, `NEEDS MATERIAL <slug>` printed above the marker, other picks continue. Never an unsupported queue status, never a partial pack marked complete.

## Worked cases

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| Source supports one rule, no diagram | Prose proceeds. No decorative pseudo-diagram. |
| Comparison has one documented alternative | Name the missing alternative, look it up within budget, else choose the simpler teaching or repair. No invented opponent data. |
| Decision card lacks a threshold | No fabricated threshold. Supported non-numeric conditions only if they resolve the decision. |
| Ordered events, no durations | Event sequence, no proportional timeline. |
| Worked example invented to show mechanics | Visibly illustrative, supported mechanics, no reported athlete result. |
| Personal story exists only in an AI summary | Attributed framing or another approach. No first-person retelling. |
| Save card needs the caption to say what it is for | Repair the title and context until it stands alone. |
| Share CTA names a colleague, body gives no joint decision | Fails share readiness. Fix the body or follow the action rules. |
| Conversation promises a resource nobody can deliver | Remove the promise or repair. No new DM system. |
| Strong lesson, no relevant product | Proceeds under a non-sales action. |
| Source-rich seed, draft omits the promised tool | Fails the final payoff check despite good source readiness. |
| Scott explicitly requests an unsupported save or format | No silent switch. Master and review state intact. Report it. |
| Automatic choice lacks ingredients | Supported alternative under the brief policy, recorded. |
| One blocked topic in a batch | Others continue. No fabricated completion, no false retirement. |
| Style-only restyle | Words and meaning unchanged. |
| Old approved deck without a receipt | Legacy. Unchanged. |
