01. Rust never sleeps

A classic Ford does not die from miles. It dies from rust. Check the cab corners, the bed seams, the floor pans. Catch it early and it is a weekend. Ignore it and it is a new panel.

02. Grease is cheap

Parts are expensive. Grease is not. Hit the zerks, pack the bearings, keep the fluids fresh. Old Fords were built to be maintained, not replaced. Reward them for it.

03. Drive it

A truck that sits is a truck that seizes. Seals dry out. Tires flat-spot. Gas goes stale. The best thing you can do for a classic Ford is put miles on it. Regularly.

04. Honor the original

Resto-mods have their place. But a numbers-matching Ford with its original drivetrain tells a story no crate motor can. When you can save the original, save it.
